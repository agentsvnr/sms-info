Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the personal text-messaging service operated by Vignesh Nadar ("Vignesh Nadar", "we", "us") through an AI assistant named Harper.

Information we collect

The service collects only the mobile phone number of its owner, the content of messages sent to and from that number, and delivery records kept by our messaging provider (Twilio).

How we use information

This information is used only to send the owner notifications, confirmations, call summaries, and verification messages, and to operate and troubleshoot the service.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Information is shared only with service providers needed to deliver messages (such as Twilio), and only to deliver those messages.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help.
