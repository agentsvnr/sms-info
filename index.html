Harper SMS Notifications

Harper is a personal AI assistant operated by Vignesh Nadar. It sends text messages only to its owner's own mobile phone, such as confirmations, call summaries, and verification messages.

How the owner opts in

The owner, Vignesh Nadar, registered the sending number, entered his own mobile number as the only recipient, and asked the assistant to text him. No one else is enrolled and no messages go to the public.

Opt-in keywords: the owner can text START, YES, or UNSTOP to the assistant's number to resume messages at any time.

Example conversation

Owner: START
Vignesh Nadar (Harper): You are now opted in to personal notifications. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.
Vignesh Nadar (Harper): Your call with the dentist is confirmed for Tuesday at 3 PM.
Owner: STOP
Harper: You have been unsubscribed. No more messages will be sent.
